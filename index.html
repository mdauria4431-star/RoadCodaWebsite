One day, start to finish

Nobody retypes anything. Every line below comes from something that already happened.

  • 06:40

    The board

    Today's loads and stops, who's on them, what's running late, and why — with the reason recorded at the time instead of reconstructed on Friday.

  • 08:12

    The driver's phone

    Arrive, scan, photograph, get the signature, depart. Three taps a stop, works with no signal, sends when it comes back.

  • 08:13

    Your customer

    An email when the truck is on the way, when it's running late, when it's delivered. A tracking link that expires after the stop.

  • 14:20

    A short pallet

    Recorded at the stop, onto the to-follow list, acknowledged by the driver. Not discovered three weeks later by a store manager.

  • 17:45

    The invoice

    Drafted the moment the load closes, priced off their contract, with tolls, detention and accessorials already on it. Weekly master invoice or per load.

  • Friday

    Driver pay

    By mile, hour, stop, load or salary, with detention and extras. Drivers see their own week in the app. Files for ADP, Paychex, Paylocity or Gusto.

  • Any day

    When something happens

    Accident and injury reports taken at the scene with photos and witness statements. OSHA logs, driver files, medical cards, claims — all in one place.

  • Quarterly

    The dull necessities

    IFTA miles and fuel, tolls reconciled against what was billed, QuickBooks export, profit by customer, route, truck and driver.

The things people ask

We already have a system.

Most are built for brokerage and charge per person, so hiring a dispatcher raises your software bill. Two questions worth asking yours: what does a second user cost, and how long does it take to prove a delivery from three weeks ago?

My drivers won't use it.

Three taps a stop, on the phone they already carry. No app store, no training day. If a driver can send a photo to his wife, he can run a route on RoadCoda.

How long before we're running on it?

One route in a week, alongside whatever you do now. Everything in two to six weeks. Your customers, stores, drivers, trucks and rates get loaded for you.

What happens to our data if we leave?

It's yours. One button exports the lot — loads, stops, photos, invoices, driver pay, safety records. We keep it for 60 days after you go, then delete it.

Who answers the phone?

The person who wrote the software. RoadCoda is one person, and you should know that before you buy rather than after. What you get for it is a direct line instead of a ticket number.
